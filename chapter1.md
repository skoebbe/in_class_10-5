# Chapter 1

In a land where magic flowed like the rivers and creatures of wonder roamed freely, there lived a goblin named Globlin. Globlin was not an ordinary goblin; he was clever and crafty, always dreaming of a life more than scavenging in the dark corners of the forest. His small stature and green skin concealed a big ambition: he wanted to steal The ORB from the powerful wizard, Keating. The ORB was a glowing sphere that held immense power, granting its holder control over time itself.

Keating was known far and wide for his vast knowledge and great spells. His tower loomed high above the village, its spire piercing the clouds. Many would tell tales of the wizard’s great achievements, and how he protected the land from darkness. However, Globlin saw this as a problem. A wizard with such power would never allow a little goblin like him to live freely. If only Globlin could get his hands on The ORB, he believed he could change his fate forever.

One day, while Globlin was rummaging through an old chest to find tools for his plan, his best friend Simon, a clever dog with a thick fur coat, dashed in. Simon loved adventures and always stood by Globlin’s side.

"What are you up to this time, Globlin?" Simon asked, tilting his head. His large, brown eyes sparkled with curiosity.

"I have a plan," Globlin said, his voice brimming with excitement. "Tonight, under the cover of darkness, we’ll sneak into Keating’s tower and steal The ORB!"
